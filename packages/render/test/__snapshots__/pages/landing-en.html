Add it in a minute

The link is the whole install: nothing to download, no account to make.

Stremio

  1. Press Install in Stremio on this page, on a computer or phone that has Stremio. No Stremio app here? Use Stremio’s website⁠ (opens in a new tab) .
  2. Or: open Addons in Stremio, paste the link into the search box and choose Install.
  3. Signed in to Stremio? It then appears in all your Stremio apps, the TV too.

Nuvio

  1. Copy the link above.
  2. In Nuvio open Settings → Addons → Add addon.
  3. Paste the link and add it. The new rows appear on the home screen.

On a TV? Typing a link with a remote is no fun: add it on your phone or computer, signed in to the same Stremio account, and it shows up on the TV too.

Keshet 12 and the home helper

Keshet 12 lets a video play only when your own home internet asks for it. So Keshet 12 needs a small home helper: a little program on a computer that stays on at home (a desktop PC or Mac, a storage box or a Raspberry Pi), which you add to your app as a second link.

Without it, Keshet 12’s shows are listed but have nothing to play, and so are 8 of the live channels. Everything else works without it.

Setting it up takes 15 minutes to an hour the first time, and that computer has to stay on. It then plays at home. All your devices share one account, so they get the same helper link: with a Cloudflare Tunnel⁠ (opens in a new tab) that one link plays at home and away.

Watching from abroad

Everything in the catalog shows up anywhere in the world. Most live channels and most of Reshet 13 play anywhere, and so do the Kan titles the broadcaster allows abroad.

A stream that plays only from an Israeli internet connection says so in the app: זמין לצפייה רק בישראל (only in Israel). That is the broadcaster’s choice, not the app’s.

Keshet 12 plays with the home helper on your home network, wherever in the world your home is. The home helper can also fetch the Israel-only titles for you, through a VPN or proxy service with an Israeli location that you may already pay for. Those titles then show a second source, דרך החיבור הישראלי שלך: pick that one.

Questions

A new episode is not there yet.

New episodes usually appear within an hour or two of going online on the channel’s site. Apps keep the rows for a while: pull down to refresh, or restart the app.

A row starts with ⚠.

That channel’s site has not been read for a while. What you see is the last good list, and it still plays. The status page says what is going on.

A row starts with ⏳.

That row is still filling (Kan’s shows arrive over a few days). The first item says how many are in so far.

Something does not play.

Check the status page, then tell us⁠ (opens in a new tab) which show and episode. If it says זמין לצפייה רק בישראל and you are abroad, that is the broadcaster’s choice.

Search finds nothing.

Search in Hebrew, the way the channel writes the title. Many well-known shows are also found by their English name.

Does it work with my other addons?

Yes. When you open an Israeli show from another addon’s rows (Stremio’s own, for example), Israel TV adds its episodes there too, if it knows the show and the seasons are numbered the same. It never touches movies.

What does it cost? Do I need an account?

Nothing, and no. There are no accounts and no tracking. The video comes straight from the broadcasters’ own free services; Israel TV hosts no video.

Open source, run by volunteers

Israel TV reads the channels’ public sites at a polite pace, gives every show and episode a permanent number, and points your app straight at the channel’s own video. The code is open: you can read it, report a problem, help fix a channel whose site changed, or run your own copy⁠ (opens in a new tab) . Licensed AGPL-3.0⁠ (opens in a new tab) .
