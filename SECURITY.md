# Security

Please report security problems privately, not in a public issue:

- **GitHub:** _Security → Report a vulnerability_ on this repository (private to the maintainers), or
- **Email:** israeltv2@proton.me

Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it affects (the public catalog, the home helper, a self-hosted copy).
We answer within a week and fix real problems in the next release; you will be credited unless you prefer not to be.

**In scope:** this repository's code, the public catalog at `israel-tv.israeltv2.workers.dev`, and the published
images. **Out of scope:** the broadcasters' own sites and services, and Cloudflare's or GitHub's infrastructure. Please
do not run load tests or brute force against the public catalog.

What Israel TV does to stay safe is described in [docs/security-audit.md](docs/security-audit.md) and
[docs/privacy.md](docs/privacy.md).

Requests to remove a show or a channel from the public addon: [docs/takedown.md](docs/takedown.md).
